# MIXHIVE Research Synthesis

## Executive Summary

MIXHIVE should be built as the AI operating system for underground music
culture, not just another social feed. The durable wedge is useful AI for
weekly creator workflows: finding opportunities, improving profiles, matching
collaborators, preparing releases, analyzing mixes, and understanding where
local scenes are moving.

VI.BE is the strongest regional benchmark because it behaves like trusted music
infrastructure. It combines opportunity access, advice, programs, and ecosystem
coordination for Flemish and Brussels artists. MIXHIVE should treat VI.BE as a
partner model and potential launch partner, not a direct competitor.

The breakthrough product is a connective intelligence layer across the tools
artists already use: SoundCloud, Bandcamp, Mixcloud, Resident Advisor, Spotify
for Artists, Discord, VI.BE, rights organizations, venues, festivals, labels,
and local collectives.

## VI.BE Deep Dive

VI.BE is a Flemish and Brussels support center for artists and the music sector,
from beginner to professional and local to international. The platform connects
artists with opportunities, organizers, contests, media exposure, showcases,
advice, trends, and ecosystem programs.

Core strengths:

- Trusted public-interest role.
- Opportunity infrastructure through organizers, calls, contests, and programs.
- Regional legitimacy and institutional credibility.
- Support for local-to-international artist growth.
- Strong program ecosystem: Sound Track, Lokale Helden, Stoemp!, MIA's,
  Belgian Music Week, Popraad, Belgium Booms.

Observed gaps MIXHIVE can complement:

- Manual opportunity search and matching.
- Limited creator-native social/collaboration UX.
- Limited audio intelligence.
- Limited explainable AI guidance.
- Limited dynamic scene mapping.
- Limited action automation across profile, booking, EPK, grant, and release workflows.

## VI.BE x MIXHIVE Collaboration Hypothesis

Shared mission: help artists grow sustainably from local scenes to wider
recognition.

MIXHIVE should position as a technological amplification layer:

- VI.BE remains trusted opportunity/institutional infrastructure.
- MIXHIVE adds creator-native AI matching, profile intelligence, audio analysis,
  collaboration matching, scene radar, and agent workflows.

Low-friction pilot:

- No private data-sharing.
- Ingest or manually seed public opportunities.
- Match opted-in MIXHIVE artist profiles to public opportunities.
- Generate weekly "Opportunity Match" reports with explainable rankings.
- Measure artist usefulness ratings and application actions.

Potential pilot projects:

- AI opportunity matcher for Flemish DJs and producers.
- AI profile/press-kit enrichment for selected artists.
- Brussels/Flanders scene map.
- Grant/subsidy application assistant.
- Sound Track or Lokale Helden AI support pilot.
- Belgium Booms export-prep EPK and pitch kit.

## Strategic Takeaway

The category gap is not another upload platform. It is an AI-native operating
layer that connects identity, music, people, places, events, funding, fan
support, and action.

The first product wedge should be the opportunity and collaboration engine,
supported by strong profile/EPK tooling and audio intelligence. That creates
weekly utility, defensible graph data, and a clear VI.BE-compatible story.
