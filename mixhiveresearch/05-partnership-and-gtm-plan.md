# MIXHIVE Partnership And GTM Plan

## Launch Thesis

Launch in Belgium/Flanders first because the ecosystem is dense, trusted,
institutional, multilingual, and opportunity-driven. Use VI.BE as the benchmark
and potential partner model. Expand later into Berlin, Amsterdam, London,
Detroit, NYC, and Tokyo only after the Belgian wedge proves utility.

## Belgian Pilot Strategy

Initial cities:

- Brussels
- Ghent
- Antwerp
- Leuven
- Liege/Charleroi as secondary Wallonia bridge

Anchor community targets:

- Kiosk Radio

- Fuse
- C12
- Ampere
- Kompass
- Chinastraat
- Beursschouwburg
- local DJ schools and rehearsal spaces

Pilot offer:

- Free Pro for anchor DJs and organizers.
- AI profile and EPK setup sessions.
- Opportunity Match weekly report.
- Scene Radar Belgian underground digest.
- Partner dashboard demos for venues/promoters.

## VI.BE Partnership Pitch

Positioning:

> MIXHIVE does not replace VI.BE. MIXHIVE adds an AI action layer that helps
> artists find the right opportunities faster, prepare stronger applications,
> improve their profiles, and understand their scene.

Pilot proposal:

- 20 to 50 opted-in artists.
- Public or consent-based opportunity matching.
- Weekly AI opportunity report.
- AI profile/EPK support.
- Match feedback survey.
- Aggregate-only results report.

Success metrics:

- Profile completion lift.
- Opportunity match usefulness score.
- Applications started.
- Applications submitted.
- Artist-reported time saved.
- Organizer-reported match quality.

## First 90 Days GTM

Weeks 1-2:

- Build demo data set with 100 hand-curated opportunities.
- Recruit 10 anchor creators.
- Produce first Belgian underground opportunity digest.

Weeks 3-4:

- Launch Profile Coach and EPK MVP to anchors.
- Collect before/after profile examples.
- Create VI.BE pitch deck from real examples.

Weeks 5-8:

- Launch Opportunity Match MVP.
- Recruit 50 creators across Brussels, Ghent, Antwerp.
- Start weekly Scene Radar digest.

Weeks 9-12:

- Pitch VI.BE and 3 venue/festival partners.
- Run co-branded or informal pilot.
- Publish first "State of Belgian Underground" report.

## Positioning Guardrails

- Human-first, AI-assisted.
- Never train generative music models on user uploads without explicit opt-in.
- No automatic spam outreach.
- Every AI recommendation explains itself.
- Local trust beats generic scale.
- MIXHIVE is infrastructure for artists and scenes, not extractive platformization.
